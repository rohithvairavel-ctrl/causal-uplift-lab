# Data

- `raw/` — full Hillstrom CSV and synthetic RCT (gitignored except `.gitkeep`; produced by `scripts/download_data.py`)
- `sample/hillstrom_sample.csv` — 2,000-row sample of the public MineThatData email challenge
- `sample/synthetic_sample.csv` — 3,000-row synthetic RCT with `true_cate`

Hillstrom source: http://www.minethatdata.com/ (Kevin Hillstrom, MineThatData Email Analytics Challenge, 2008).
